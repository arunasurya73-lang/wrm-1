Health & Daily Smog Protection Guide

VULNERABLE GROUPS
HIGH PRIORITY

Children & Seniors

Lungs & Heart Protection
  • Keep Inhalers Handy: Keep rescue inhalers and SpO2 pulse oximeters readily accessible at all times.
  • Avoid Peak Smog Hours: Avoid outdoor walks and workouts before 10 AM & after 6 PM during heavy smog.
⚠️ Emergency: Seek immediate medical care if severe breathlessness or chest tightness occurs.
INDOOR PROTECTION
MUST FOLLOW
🏠

Home & Indoor Defense

Air Purification & Smog Sealing
  • Use Wet Mopping: Use damp mops instead of dry brooms to avoid stirring up fine PM10 dust.
  • Optimal Purifier Placement: Place air purifiers 1 foot away from walls with doors and windows closed.
💡 Pro Tip: Clean air purifier pre-filters every 15 days for maximum clean air delivery.
COMMUTE & TRAVEL
DAILY PROTOCOL
🚗

Daily Travel & Transit

Vehicle AC & Mask Protocol
  • Turn On AC Recirculation: Keep car AC on internal air mode to block road exhaust fumes from entering.
  • Wear Certified N95 Mask: Always wear a snug N95 mask when traveling on two-wheelers or open transit.
🛡️ Rule: Turn off your engine at traffic signals exceeding 15 seconds to cut localized pollution.
